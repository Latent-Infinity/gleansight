## Fin-JEPA: Joint-Embedding Predictive Representation Learning for Financial Time Series

## Abstract

We present Fin-JEPA, the first application of the Joint-Embedding Predictive Architecture (JEPA) to financial time series. Fin-JEPA learns 64-dimensional latent representations of daily equity features through a lightweight 367K-parameter architecture comprising a PriceEncoder (MLP with GELU activations, LayerNorm) and a TransformerPredictor with 4-layer causal Transformer ( n\_heads=4 , embed\_dim=64 ). Representational collapse is prevented through Sketched Isotropic Gaussian Regularization (SIGReg, λ = 0 . 1). On a large-scale dataset of 6,230 publicly traded equities spanning 2010-2026 (2.07M training samples), the training loss converges from 1.369 to 0.706 over 50 epochs with no collapse ( stdZ =1.35, eff\_rank =37.8). The learned latent predictions outperform an identity baseline by 10.1% ( Z\_pred MSE0.2922 vs. 0.3249), confirming that daily financial time series contain learnable temporal structure. In downstream evaluation, we analyze prediction error (VoE) as a potential trading signal across 300 stocks (393K windows), finding weak-to-negligible signal (AUC ≈ 0.49, Spearman r ≈ 0). We outline future work for PELT-based regime detection and multi-horizon training.
